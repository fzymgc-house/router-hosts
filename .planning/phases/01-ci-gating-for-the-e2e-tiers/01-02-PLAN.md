<planner_decisions>
Two design points RESEARCH.md left open are decided here, explicitly, so the
executor does not re-open them:

**Decision P-01 — `helpers_test.go`'s bind-retry loop routes through the helper.**
RESEARCH.md flagged this as the one structurally different site (it retries an
*action*, `net.Listen`, rather than polling a passive read). Decision: convert
it. The condition closure performs the listen attempt and assigns the resulting
listener via closure capture; the timeout is `maxBindAttempts * bindRetryDelay`
(20 x 100ms = 2s), preserving today's budget exactly. Rationale: it is a bounded
wait for a port to become bindable, D-13 explicitly routes poll intervals inside
already-bounded loops *through* the helper, and the conversion strictly improves
the failure path (today a bind that never succeeds reaches `require.NoError`
with the last error; after conversion `wait.Until` fatals with a description
naming the address). If `golangci-lint` rejects the closure capture, the fallback
is to keep the loop AND give its `time.Sleep` a `SLEEP-INTENTIONAL:` marker with
a one-line justification — but record that as a deviation in the SUMMARY,
because it is the fallback, not the plan.

**Decision P-02 — `waitForDockerServer`'s log-dump side effect stays a thin
wrapper (implemented in plan 03).** `wait.Until` does not grow a failure hook.
Recorded here for completeness; plan 03 owns the implementation.
</planner_decisions>
