<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** `pluralization` on "also" — "…blocked until `docker_e2e` and `proc_e2e`
have **also** run and passed…". One e2e tier ran in CI before this phase; three
will after it.

**Primary noun (generalized identity):** *the set of e2e tiers*. Not "the e2e
gate" (singular), and not "the fast tier plus two extras".

**Decision: `promote`.** The aggregator is no longer treated as a hand-maintained
list that happens to mention three e2e jobs. `internal/ciwiring`'s invariant test
promotes the generalized identity to primary by asserting **set-equality**
between `{ top-level jobs whose id matches ^e2e- }` and `{ jobs represented in
ci-go-complete's needs AND its result-check block }`. The old specific identity
("the fast tier is the e2e gate") is demoted to one member of that set with no
privileged status in the wiring.

**Rationale:** without this, adding a fourth tier and forgetting the aggregator
line produces exactly the failure CI-01 exists to rule out — a tier that runs,
reports, and gates nothing. The invariant makes "a tier exists" and "a tier
gates" the same fact.

**Consistency with locked D-01:** the promotion is at the *representation* layer
(how the workflow describes its own tier set), not at the required-check layer.
The required-check set stays exactly `CI (Go) Complete` + `Vulnerability check`,
the `protect-main` ruleset is untouched, and no `E2E Complete` context is minted.
D-01 is honored in full.

**Demonstrated red requirement:** per D-16/CI-03 this invariant is itself a gate
written by this phase, so it must be observed failing before acceptance — see
task 2's acceptance criteria.
</assumption_delta_decision>
