<objective>
Bound the heap for `ExportHosts`, the phase's remaining named consumer, and take D-04's three
free wins.

D-03 is the change that actually removes the O(N entries) peak this phase claims: today the
`json` and `csv` branches build `entries`, then `out []jsonEntry` or a `bytes.Buffer`, then
`data []byte` — three full materializations of the inventory before `sendExportChunks` frames
a single byte. TMPL-06's chunking bounded the WIRE; this bounds the HEAP.

D-02 keeps `hosts` materialized and says so out loud. Only `hosts` sorts — `FormatHostsFile`
is the single global sort in the export path, and D-01a corrected the roadmap's claim that
`json` sorts too. `json` and `csv` never sorted, which is precisely what makes streaming them
possible without a two-pass key sort.

Purpose: this is the plan the LAZY-02 benchmark measures. If the three materializations
survive, there is nothing for the benchmark to show.

Output: streaming `json`/`csv`, a documented descoped `hosts`, a page-counted hook count, and
two in-package filters that no longer drain the world.
</objective>
